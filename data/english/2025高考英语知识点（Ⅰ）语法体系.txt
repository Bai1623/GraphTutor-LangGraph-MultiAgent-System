高考英语知识点（一）—— 语法体系（8大核心板块）

2025年趋势：语法不再单独命题，而是在写作、完形、语法填空等题型中考查实际运用能力。复习重点从"记规则"转向"在语境中识别和运用"。

一、时态与语态

高考高频时态对比：
- 一般现在时 vs 一般过去时：now/every day vs yesterday/last week
- 现在完成时（have/has done）：与现在有联系，关键词since/for/already/yet/in the past few years
- 过去完成时（had done）：过去的过去，关键词by the time/before+过去时间
- 现在完成进行时（have been doing）：从过去持续到现在还在进行
- 将来时表达：will do/be going to/be about to/be to do

被动语态各种时态：
- 一般现在被动：am/is/are done
- 一般过去被动：was/were done
- 现在完成被动：have/has been done
- 情态动词被动：can/must/should be done

二、非谓语动词（高频考点，语法填空必考）

不定式（to do）：
- 作主语：To learn English well is important. → It is important to learn English well.
- 作宾语：want/decide/hope/refuse/agree to do
- 作目的状语：He got up early to catch the bus.
- 作结果状语（意外）：He hurried to the station, only to find the train had left.
- 不带to的不定式：make/let/have sb do；had better/would rather do

动名词（doing）：
- 作主语：Reading is my hobby.
- 作宾语：enjoy/mind/suggest/avoid/practice doing
- 固定搭配：look forward to doing/be used to doing/pay attention to doing
- remember doing（做过）vs remember to do（要去做）

分词（doing/done）：
- 现在分词表主动/进行：The boy sitting there is my brother.
- 过去分词表被动/完成：The book written by Lu Xun is famous.
- 分词作状语：Having finished his homework, he went out. (主动+先于谓语)
- 独立主格：Weather permitting, we will go out.

三、三大从句

定语从句（修饰名词）：
- 关系代词：who/whom/whose/that/which
- 关系副词：when/where/why
- 非限制性定语从句（有逗号）：which可指代整个主句
- 介词+关系代词：in which/for which/of which
- 高频考点：where引导的定语从句（先行词为抽象地点如situation/case/point/stage）

名词性从句（充当名词）：
- 主语从句：What he said is true./It is obvious that...
- 宾语从句：I believe (that) he is honest./I don't know whether/if...
- 表语从句：The question is whether we should go.（注意whether不用if）
- 同位语从句：There is no doubt that.../The news that he won surprised us.
- 考点：that vs what——that不充当成分，what充当主语/宾语

状语从句（修饰动词）：
- 时间：when/while/as/before/after/since/until/the moment
- 条件：if/unless/as long as/provided that
- 让步：although/though/even though/while
- 原因：because/since/as/now that
- 结果：so...that/such...that
- as/though倒装结构：Young as he is, he knows a lot./Try as he might, he failed.

四、虚拟语气

if条件句虚拟：
- 与现在相反：If I were you, I would study harder.
- 与过去相反：If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.
- 与将来相反：If it were to rain tomorrow, we would cancel the trip.
- 错综时间：If I had taken your advice then, I wouldn't be in trouble now.（过去→现在）

名词性从句中的虚拟：
- suggest/insist/recommend/order后的that从句：(should) do
- It is necessary/important that... (should) do
- wish后的虚拟：wish I were/wish I had done/wish I could do

五、特殊句式

倒装句：
- 部分倒装（助动词提前）：Not only...but also/Never/Seldom/Hardly...when
- Only+状语开头：Only in this way can we succeed.
- So/Such开头：So beautiful is the girl that everyone likes her.

强调句：It is/was...that...（去掉后句子仍然完整）
- It was in the park that I met him.（强调地点）
- It was because he was ill that he didn't come.（强调原因）
- 与定语从句区别：强调句去掉It is...that后句子完整

省略句：While (I was) waiting, I read a book.
